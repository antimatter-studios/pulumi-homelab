- `provider/safeops`: one shared way for a delete to remove an entry only while it is still exactly what a resource made. It pins the parent directory, captures the entry with `rename(2)` into a private directory, and checks it there. It then removes the entry, or puts it back without replacing anything (`link(2)`, or `renameat2(RENAME_NOREPLACE)` for a directory). It reports what it kept and where, for the caller to turn into a warning. It runs as a perl script, since `perl-base` is Essential on Debian, and refuses on a machine without perl.
