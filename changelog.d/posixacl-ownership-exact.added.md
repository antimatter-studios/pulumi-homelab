- `PosixAcl` can declare the owning `user::`, `group::` and `other::` access entries (on a file, its mode bits), the path's `owner` and `group` (chowned, and read back by name from getfacl's header), and `exact: true`, under which named entries that are not declared are removed and one appearing later is drift. All are optional, and a declaration that uses none of them behaves as before. Nothing reads or writes the path's content, so a secret written by something else can have its permissions declared without passing through the program or the state.
